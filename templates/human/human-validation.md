Do not label a character interchangeable from structural tests alone. Record the
exact poses/actions, proportion profiles, equipment configurations, frame range,
contact tolerances and failures. Unreachable targets fail; they are not silently
solved by stretching the body or editing approved equipment. Unsupported tests
remain NOT RUN and restrict release/compatibility claims.
